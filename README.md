# **Healthcare Analytics: Emergency Room Patient and Clinic Reports Analysis**


## **Overview**
This project analyzes emergency room visit data using Power BI to uncover insights into patient demographics, visit types, satisfaction levels, and referral patterns. The dashboard is designed to provide actionable insights for optimizing healthcare delivery and enhancing patient care experiences.

---

## **Objectives**
1. **Track Average Wait Times**:
   - Analyze how long patients typically wait before their appointments.
   - Uncover patterns and trends to assess healthcare system efficiency.

2. **Patient Satisfaction**:
   - Evaluate average satisfaction scores and identify contributing factors for a better patient experience.

3. **Monthly Patient Visits**:
   - Visualize the dynamics of patient volumes over time.

4. **Administrative vs. Non-Administrative Appointments**:
   - Understand the impact of administrative processes on wait times and satisfaction levels.

5. **Referrals vs. Walk-In Patients**:
   - Analyze the distribution of referrals and walk-ins to measure their influence on patient care.

6. **Demographic Insights**:
   - Explore patient visits segmented by age groups and races to understand diverse healthcare needs.

---

## **Features**
### **Metrics Included**
- **Average Wait Time**: Detailed insights into waiting time trends.
- **Patient Satisfaction**: Visualized patient feedback analysis.
- **Visit Trends**: Monthly fluctuations in patient visits.
- **Appointment Type Analysis**: Differentiation between administrative and non-administrative appointments.
- **Referrals & Walk-Ins**: Proportions and impacts on patient flow.
- **Demographics**: Age group and race distributions.

## **Steps for Implementation**

### **1. Data Collection**
The dataset spans from **2019 to 2020** and includes:
- Archive Dates.
- Specialties and Demographics (Age, Race).
- Waiting Times and Satisfaction Scores.

### **2. Data Cleaning**
- Ensured consistent date formats.
- Removed duplicates and filled missing values.
- Validated CSV formatting for compatibility.

### **3. Data Transformation**
- Utilized **Power BI Power Query** for:
  - Filtering unnecessary data.
  - Creating calculated columns (e.g., Average Wait Time, Satisfaction Scores).

### **4. Dashboard Design**
- Created a **Visualization Blueprint** to map:
  - KPIs and metrics.
  - Interactive charts and graphs.

### **5. Publishing**
- Published the dashboard on **GitHub** for collaboration.
- Enabled user interactivity with slicers and drill-through options.
